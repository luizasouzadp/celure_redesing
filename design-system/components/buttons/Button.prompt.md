The only call-to-action shape in Celure: a fully rounded pill with a tiny sparkle glyph — use primary on photography/dark sections, secondary inside cards and forms.
```jsx
<Button href="#contato">Planejar Minha Viagem</Button>
<Button variant="secondary" fullWidth>Cotar Passagens</Button>
```
- `variant`: primary (white) | secondary (brand-green)
- `fullWidth`: label left, glyph right — used on mobile and in TripCard
- `icon={false}` for text-only (cookie banner "Recusar")
